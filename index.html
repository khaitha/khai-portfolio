About Me
The person behind the work.

I'm a digital media specialist with a genuine love for the craft of vector illustration. There's something satisfying about pulling a bezier handle and watching a shape click into place. That's the part I actually enjoy.

My toolkit is mostly open-source: Inkscape for vector work, GIMP and Krita for raster and digital painting, LibreSprite for pixel art, and Blender when something needs a third dimension.

Right now I'm available for remote contract work, especially projects involving AI training assets, digital illustration, and character design.
